# Channels = OS-essential, not designed

* Keep channels as essential as possible — OS vibe

* Like Finder treats folders: no chrome, no narrative, no marketing copy on rows

* The folder IS the surface; nothing decorating it

* Mac sidebar shows folders as a flat indented list — that's the reference, not Slack's channel list

* Read alongside \[\[2026-05-19-layman-folder-ui]] — refines the visual direction of that pitch

Questions:

* What does "essential" mean concretely? No icons? Just names? Mono spacing?

* Does the row hold metadata at all (unread count, last-activity time), or pure name + nothing?

* Hover affordances ok, or even those are noise?

* Is there a precedent — Plan 9, Finder, OS X column view — that nails this vibe?

* Where does the AI assistant attach itself visually? Inline with the folder, or in a panel that opens on click?

Don't shape yet.
