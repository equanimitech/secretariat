# Stamp comprehension gate

Pitch — 2026-05-21. Source: conversation 2026-05-21 (no captured idea file; arose from Rafa's reaction to Manu-handoff draft).

## Boundaries

### Job to be done

When the principal is about to stamp an envelope, I want the ceremony to force a brief act of comprehension — distinct from the Touch ID reflex — so that the stamped subset of traffic actually represents *attested meaning* and not muscle-memory clicks. Baseline today: Touch ID + headline-in-dialog + show-body-first contract (AGENTS.md rule #4). That contract defeats phishing (Claude shows X, stamps Y) but is silent on self-deception (principal sees body, eyes glaze, finger presses). The threat surface widens as stamp volume grows; if stamps inflate, every stamp's evidential weight collapses.

### Appetite

`medium`

> Appetite picked: `medium` — three surfaces (cognition port + MCP tool flow + Tauri UI), no lexicon edit, no domain change. Override with `--appetite=<size>`.

## Elements

Two interventions, both client-side. **No lexicon edit, no on-wire change.** The stamp record (`tech.equanimi.secretariat.stamp`) is unchanged; comprehension is a property of the stamping ceremony, not of the record.

* **Place:** stamp confirmation surface (MCP `stamp` tool + Tauri stamp button).

  * **Affordance:** read-back probe — after the body is shown, the surface asks ONE open question requiring re-reading (e.g. *"In your own words, what does this envelope commit you to?"*). Free-text answer routed through `CognitionPort` for semantic match against the body. Match → Touch ID fires. Mismatch → re-display body, no Touch ID dialog. Cannot be gamed by reflex because the answer space is open.

  * **Connection:** principal types/speaks → cognition adapter judges → on-pass, Touch ID dialog opens with the existing headline+hash. On-fail, surface shows *what the principal said* next to *what the body says*, principal re-reads, retries or aborts.

* **Place:** stamp button affordance (Tauri envelope view, `crates/secretariat-ui/src/...` stamp footer at `crates/core/src/domain/envelope.rs` adjacent surface).

  * **Affordance:** **soft default → signed-only.** The primary action on a draft is *"send (signed)"*, not *"stamp & send"*. Stamping is a secondary action behind a small toggle/menu, accompanied by inline consequences copy: *"signed only is enough for most messages. stamping will lock the content (no editing after) and commit your voice to it."* The copy fades in over ~1.2s when the toggle is revealed (render-dwell, not body-dwell) — micro-animation forces the eye to land on the warning instead of skipping pre-rendered text. The stamp affordance is non-interactive during the reveal.

  * **Connection:** principal must opt the envelope INTO the stamp tier; default flow is signed-only ambient traffic. Per rule #4 the substrate already supports this — UI just makes the rare path feel rare. Consequences copy anchors anticipated regret (BCT 5.5) and salience of consequences (BCT 5.2) in *concrete material irreversibility* (no editing after stamp) rather than abstract ledger weight.

* **Place:** CLI `sec stamp` (no cognition session available).

  * **Affordance:** **dwell-time fallback** — stamp button (Touch ID prompt) disabled for `max(5s, body_word_count / 4 wps)`. Weaker than the probe but cheap; reflects evidence from consent-dwell-time research. CLI is the power-user surface; weaker gate is acceptable there.

* **Place:** probe input field (MCP `stamp` flow + Tauri probe modal).

  * **Affordance:** **anti-circumvention constraints** — paste is disabled in the answer field; copy from the envelope body is disabled while the probe is active; the probe modal is focus-locked (tab-switch / app-switch → probe invalidates, body re-displays, principal restarts). Goal: force the answer to be typed from the principal's own head, not laundered through another Claude/LLM session.

  * **Connection:** these are *soft* gates — defense in depth, not absolute. A determined principal can always look at the screen and dictate to a phone. The point is to remove the *frictionless* circumvention paths so the easy thing is the honest thing. Residual risk is acknowledged; v1 doesn't try to close it past the obvious vectors.

## Risks

### 🐇 Rabbit holes

* **Semantic match judgment quality.** What's the threshold for "the principal's answer reflects the body"? Too strict → users frustrated (re-prompted on valid paraphrases). Too loose → reflex answers slip through (*"I commit to the thing"*). Need a small calibration set of {body, good-answer, lazy-answer} triples to tune. **Figureoutable** — Claude can self-evaluate against the corpus with a held-out set; if false-positive rate > 20% in pilot, fall back to a checklist-style probe (3 dynamic multi-choice generated by Claude from the body) before abandoning the open-question shape.

* **Where the probe lives in the cognition layer.** `CognitionPort` exists but is currently shaped around routing/launching/session (per recent `refactor(ports/cognition)` commit `c4651ee`). The probe is a synchronous in-line LLM call. May need a new `comprehension_check` method on the port, or it threads through the existing session. **Figureoutable** — read `crates/core/src/ports/cognition/` and see which sibling fits.

* **Voice vs text answer mode.** If voice ever ships, the probe gets stronger (read-back ceremony, like a notary). Text-only is fine for v1 but design the port boundary to not foreclose voice. **Easy** — port returns `ComprehensionVerdict` regardless of input modality.

* **LLM-laundering the probe.** The probe asks the principal to demonstrate comprehension *without* Claude's help — but the principal can paste the body into a different Claude session (or any LLM) and paste the generated answer back. If this becomes the default behavior, the probe is theater. Mitigations in element 4 (paste-disabled, copy-disabled, focus-lock) close the obvious vectors but not the screen-reading + second-device vector. **Partially figureoutable** — the soft gates ship in v1; the residual gap is a *trust contract* with the principal, not a technical control. If pilot reveals that the principal naturally reaches for a second LLM, the pitch has failed and we re-shape (likely toward voice-only or a different intervention shape entirely).

### 🏴 Off-sides called

* **Stamp tiers / new** **`act: ceremony`** **lexicon value.** Initial sketch wanted to extend the lexicon's `act` enum. Cut: ceremony is HOW the stamp is gated, not WHAT it records. The on-wire act stays `attest`. Comprehension belongs to the client, not the substrate.

* **Stamp budget / running ledger UI.** *"this is your 12th stamp this week"* visibility was sketched. Real but separate concern — it's about portfolio awareness, not single-envelope comprehension. Belongs in a later pitch on stamp inflation telemetry.

* **Counter-stamp ceremony.** Out of scope per AGENTS.md rule #4 — that's v0.4+ (m.3 process-verbaux). Comprehension gate is single-principal.

* **Re-gating already-stamped envelopes.** No retroactive comprehension check. Past stamps are past.

### 🥩 Fat cut

* **Dwell-time fallback for MCP/Tauri.** Drop. Only CLI uses it (no cognition session). MCP/Tauri have a probe; layering dwell + probe is friction theater.

* **Configurable probe prompt template.** Drop for v1. Ship one Claude-authored probe. Configurability returns once we see what real probes look like in practice.

* **A/B test the probe wording.** Drop. We have one principal-class user (Rafa, Marcelo); test in-vivo with conversation, not telemetry.

### 🧪 Domain knowledge

* **Verify with Rafa: is signed-only the right default for the Tauri surface?** Today the UI doesn't strongly distinguish send-signed from send-stamped — the entire compose flow assumes stamping. If signed-only becomes the default action, that's a semantic shift for the existing user (Rafa) that needs explicit confirmation. Could change the felt character of the tool.

* **Verify with Marcelo (book): does "comprehension gate" map onto a named pattern in the Autonomous Enterprise framework?** Sole-author owns the framework (memory: project\_marcelo\_book\_authorship); this pitch may surface or contradict a chapter primitive. Worth a stamped envelope to Marcelo asking *"does this rhyme with anything you've written?"* before building.

* **Pilot with one real stamp moment, not a synthetic one.** First test: the next time Rafa stamps an envelope to Marcelo for real, run the probe manually (Claude asks in-chat) and see whether the answer feels like meaningful re-reading or like an extra hoop. Anecdote first; ship second.

## Pitch

### Problem

The stamp ceremony today has a strong cryptographic gate (Touch ID, biometric, key never leaves device) and a strong anti-phishing gate (headline-in-dialog + show-body-first contract). It has *no* comprehension gate. Watching real stamping flows, the principal-side failure mode isn't *"Claude tricked me"* — it's *"I scrolled past the body, the dialog popped, my finger went to the sensor, done."* This is the T\&C problem: every gate designed as a checkbox eventually becomes a reflex.

Without a comprehension gate, the stamped subset of traffic drifts toward indistinguishability from the unstamped subset. The whole proposition of Secretariat — *the stamped subset IS the authoritative decision ledger* (rule #4) — depends on stamps carrying evidential weight. If Rafa stamps 30 envelopes/day reflexively, his stamps are worth no more than his signatures. The architectural moat erodes from inside, not from a phishing attack.

The fix is not more friction on each stamp. More friction becomes its own reflex. The fix is *scarcity by default* (most traffic flows signed-only; the UI nudges away from stamping ambient envelopes) *combined with* a probe on the stamps that do fire (an open question whose answer space is too large to reflex through).

### The bet

Medium appetite. Ship the probe + the signed-only-by-default UI shift, behind a single flag for the pilot. Two weeks of dogfood (Rafa + Marcelo correspondence on the book). Success metric is qualitative, not quantitative: at the end of two weeks, does Rafa feel that the stamps he applied represent *moments he meant*, vs. the current baseline where the stamp is a near-automatic step in the send flow? If yes — promote. If the probe feels like a hoop — re-pitch with a different gate (likely the checklist variant from the rabbit-hole list).

The bet pays off because it strengthens the *only* property of Secretariat that matters at scale: *attested meaning is rare and trustworthy*. Every other invariant in AGENTS.md (no central server, no telemetry, keys local) protects against external threats. This is the only one protecting against the principal's own reflexes — which is the threat that grows the most with use.

### No-gos

* No attempt to airtight-defend against second-device LLM-laundering of the probe (acknowledged residual risk; soft gates only).
* No lexicon edit. The on-wire stamp record stays exactly as it is.

* No domain change in `crates/core/src/domain/stamp.rs`. Comprehension is client-layer.

* No new `StampAct` variant. The act recorded is still `attest`.

* No retroactive comprehension on past stamps.

* No counter-stamp / multi-party machinery (v0.4+, separate pitch).

* No telemetry on probe pass/fail rates (rule: no telemetry, ever).

* No configurable probe prompt in v1 — ship one and learn.
